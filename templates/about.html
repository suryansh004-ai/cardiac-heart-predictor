💡 Why High Recall Matters in Cardiac Risk Screening:

In clinical prediction systems, False Negatives (predicting a patient is healthy when they are actually at high risk) are far more dangerous than False Positives. Logistic Regression achieves 90.91% recall, ensuring the vast majority of at-risk patients are detected and advised for further medical checkups.

This project utilizes the benchmark UCI Cleveland Heart Disease dataset, containing 303 patient records with 14 attributes collected by medical researchers.

4. Preprocessing & Anti-Leakage Methodology

Strict Featurization Order

A common mistake in beginner ML projects is fitting the scaler on the entire dataset before splitting. In this project, StandardScaler is fitted strictly on the training split (\(X_{\text{train}}\)) and then used to transform both \(X_{\text{train}}\) and \(X_{\text{test}}\), eliminating data leakage.

Stratified Train/Test Split

An 80/20 train/test split was configured with stratify=y and a fixed seed (random_state=42) to ensure reproducible class distribution in both splits.

Inference Pipeline Parity

When user inputs are received in the Flask application, the exact same saved StandardScaler is applied before feeding the array into model.predict() and model.predict_proba(), guaranteeing 100% pipeline consistency.
